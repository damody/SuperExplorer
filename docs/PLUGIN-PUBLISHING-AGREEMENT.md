## 1. Parties, acceptance, and controlling text

This Plugin Publishing Agreement (the **Agreement**) is between Damody (**Publisher**) and the author or rights-holding entity that submits a Plugin for an official channel (**Author**). It becomes binding when both parties sign or affirmatively accept an identified version through an approved electronic workflow. The three language versions have the same intended meaning; the [Simplified Chinese version](PLUGIN-PUBLISHING-AGREEMENT.zh-CN.md) controls any conflict.

## 2. Appointment and channels

Author appoints Publisher on a non-exclusive basis to review, package, market, distribute, sell, deliver, update, and support the accepted Plugin through Publisher's Steamworks account and other official SuperExplorer storefronts or channels (**Official Channels**). Valve and other platforms are not parties to this Agreement and do not owe Author a revenue share. Author may distribute the Plugin elsewhere if that distribution complies with the [Plugin SDK License](PLUGIN-SDK-LICENSE.md) and does not conflict with an agreed promotion or platform restriction.

## 3. Ownership and publishing license

Author retains copyright in Author's original Plugin. Author grants Publisher a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-bearing as described below, transferable, sublicensable license during the Term to reproduce, host, encode, package, translate, demonstrate, advertise, market, distribute, sell, make available, and support the Plugin through Official Channels.

The license includes the continuing rights reasonably necessary to preserve customer downloads, verify ownership, issue refunds, provide security or compatibility fixes, and support purchasers after suspension or termination. Publisher may modify the Plugin only for packaging, localization, storefront presentation, security, legal compliance, interoperability, compatibility, and maintenance continuity. Publisher will identify material Publisher-made changes where practical.

## 4. Submission, review, and release

Author shall provide complete source, build instructions, metadata, supported versions, capabilities, dependencies, bundled tools, security information, age/content disclosures, artwork, localization, support contact, and third-party license notices reasonably requested by Publisher. Publisher may test, reject, request changes, delay, price, discount, bundle, feature, suspend, or delist a Plugin in good faith for quality, security, compatibility, legal, commercial, or platform reasons. Submission does not guarantee release or minimum sales.

## 5. Net Revenue

**Gross Receipts** means amounts actually received by Publisher from sales or paid access attributable to the Plugin through Official Channels. **Net Revenue** means Gross Receipts less:

1. Steam or other platform commissions, transaction and payment-processing fees;
2. refunds, returns, credits, discounts, coupons, chargebacks, fraud losses, and bad debt;
3. sales, value-added, goods-and-services, withholding, and similar taxes collected, remitted, or legally required to be withheld; and
4. direct third-party distribution charges approved in writing by Author.

Publisher's general overhead, SuperExplorer development cost, and ordinary marketing labor are not deductions unless separately approved in writing.

## 6. Revenue share

Publisher shall allocate **90% of Net Revenue to Author** and **10% of Net Revenue to SuperExplorer/Publisher**. The split is calculated per reporting period after the permitted deductions. No revenue is earned on free copies, complimentary keys, fraudulent transactions, refunded transactions, or taxes that Publisher does not retain.

## 7. Statements and payment

Calendar quarters end March 31, June 30, September 30, and December 31. Publisher shall provide a statement and pay the undisputed amount due within **45 days after quarter-end**, subject to receiving the relevant platform report and valid payment and tax information.

Balances below **US$100** carry forward without interest until the threshold is reached. Publisher may convert non-USD receipts using the platform settlement amount or the actual commercially reasonable conversion rate applied to Publisher. After termination and completion of refund reserves, Publisher shall pay the final undisputed balance even if below US$100. Author must dispute a statement with reasonable detail within 12 months after delivery.

## 8. Taxes and payment information

Author is responsible for Author's income, business, and other taxes. Publisher may withhold and remit amounts required by law and shall provide available supporting documentation. Author shall provide accurate identity, banking, residency, and tax forms and promptly update them. Publisher may hold payment, without forfeiture, while required information is missing or sanctions, fraud, ownership, or compliance review is unresolved.

## 9. Author maintenance and support

Author shall provide commercially reasonable customer support, compatibility maintenance, and correction of reproducible material defects. Critical security or privacy issues must be acknowledged promptly and remediated within a period reasonable for their severity. Author shall maintain buildable source and cooperate with platform and SuperExplorer updates.

## 10. Maintenance failure and continuity

If Author stops responding, discontinues maintenance, cannot perform, or fails to remedy a serious issue after reasonable notice, Publisher may modify the Plugin, appoint another maintainer, suspend new sales, issue an update, roll back a release, or delist the Plugin. Publisher's continuity rights do not transfer Plugin copyright. Net Revenue remains shared under this Agreement for continued paid sales, including materially maintained versions, unless the parties sign a replacement arrangement.

## 11. Pricing, refunds, and customer obligations

Publisher controls Official Channel pricing, discounts, regional pricing, and refund administration after reasonable consultation where practical. Platform-mandated refunds and consumer remedies apply. Existing customers may retain access and download rights required by platform rules, consumer law, and this Agreement even after delisting.

## 12. Author warranties

Author represents that Author has authority to enter this Agreement; owns or has sufficient rights to all submitted code, branding, media, data, dependencies, and tools; has disclosed relevant licenses and restrictions; and that the Plugin does not knowingly infringe rights, contain malware, conceal material behavior, unlawfully collect data, or violate platform policies. Author shall disclose material AI-generated content and applicable restrictions.

## 13. Claims and indemnity

Author shall defend and indemnify Publisher and its distributors against third-party claims, losses, and reasonable costs arising from Author's breach of Section 12, unlawful Plugin behavior, or Author's tax obligations, provided Publisher promptly notifies Author and allows reasonable control of the defense. Publisher may participate with its own counsel. Author need not accept a settlement that admits Publisher fault; Author may not settle in a way that imposes non-monetary obligations on Publisher without consent.

## 14. Confidentiality and customer data

Each party shall protect non-public business, technical, security, sales, and customer information using reasonable care and use it only for this Agreement. Confidentiality does not cover information lawfully public, already known without duty, independently developed, or lawfully received from another source. Required disclosure is permitted after notice where lawful. Author receives only customer data that Publisher and the platform may lawfully share and must follow applicable privacy and security requirements.

## 15. Records and audit

Publisher shall retain records reasonably supporting statements for at least three years. No more than once per calendar year, Author may have an independent accountant inspect only relevant records on 20 business days' notice, subject to confidentiality and at Author's cost. Publisher shall reimburse reasonable audit cost if a final audit finds an underpayment exceeding 5% for the audited period, in addition to paying the shortfall.

## 16. Term and termination

This Agreement continues until either party gives 30 days' written notice, unless an agreed release commitment states otherwise. Either party may terminate for a material breach not cured within 15 days after notice. Publisher may suspend immediately for credible security, infringement, sanctions, fraud, platform, or legal risk.

Termination ends new publishing rights except as required for wind-down, refunds, accounting, legal compliance, security, and existing-customer continuity. Accrued payment, ownership, confidentiality, warranties, indemnity, records, liability, dispute, and necessary customer-support provisions survive.

## 17. Disclaimer and limitation of liability

Except for express obligations, Official Channels and publishing services are provided “as is.” Neither party guarantees sales, ranking, availability, compatibility, or uninterrupted platform operation. To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, punitive, or consequential loss. Each party's aggregate liability is limited to amounts paid or payable to Author in the preceding 12 months. The cap does not apply to payment obligations, fraud, willful misconduct, indemnity, confidentiality breach, infringement, or liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

## 18. Governing law and disputes

This Agreement is governed by PRC law. After written notice and 30 days for informal resolution, disputes shall be finally resolved by one-arbitrator CIETAC arbitration seated in Beijing, conducted in Chinese under the rules effective when filed. English evidence may be submitted unless translation is required. Non-waivable consumer protections and small-claims remedies remain available. Either party may seek urgent court relief in any jurisdiction for infringement, unauthorized distribution, confidentiality breach, or preservation of evidence.

## 19. General terms and contact

The parties are independent contractors; this Agreement creates no employment, partnership, fiduciary, franchise, or agency relationship. Neither party may bind the other. Author may not assign this Agreement without Publisher's written consent; Publisher may assign it with SuperExplorer, the publishing business, or substantially all relevant assets. Amendments must be in an attributable writing accepted by both parties. If a provision is unenforceable, it is limited or severed to the minimum necessary. Questions and notices may be submitted through the [SuperExplorer GitHub repository](https://github.com/damody/SuperExplorer) or another recorded channel agreed by the parties.
